# New Employee Onboarding Policy

**Document ID:** POL-ONB-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All new hires and their managers

## 1. Pre-Start Preparation

HR sends new hires a welcome packet **1 week before their start date**, including:
first-day logistics, IT setup instructions, and required paperwork (I-9, W-4 or local
equivalent, direct deposit form).

## 2. First Day

New hires should report to their assigned office (or join a virtual welcome session for
remote hires) at **9:00 AM local time**. IT will provision a laptop, email account, and
access to core systems (HR portal, Slack, project management tools) before the first day
wherever possible.

## 3. 30-60-90 Day Plan

Managers are required to create a 30-60-90 day plan for each new hire, covering:

- **Days 1–30**: Orientation, tool access, shadowing, initial training modules.
- **Days 31–60**: Ownership of small tasks/projects with close manager support.
- **Days 61–90**: Increasing independence, first performance check-in.

## 4. Mandatory Training

All new hires must complete the following within their first **2 weeks**:

1. Information Security Awareness training (see POL-SEC-001).
2. Code of Conduct acknowledgment (see POL-COC-001).
3. Anti-Harassment training.

Completion is tracked in the HR portal and required for continued system access beyond
30 days.

## 5. Probationary Period

New hires are subject to a **90-day introductory period**, during which performance and
fit are evaluated more closely. This period does not change at-will employment status
where applicable.

## 6. Buddy Program

Each new hire is paired with a peer "buddy" (not their manager) for their first 90 days
to help with informal questions and cultural onboarding.
