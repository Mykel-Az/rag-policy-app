# Employee Benefits Policy

**Document ID:** POL-BEN-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All full-time employees

## 1. Health Insurance

Full-time employees (30+ hours/week) are eligible for medical, dental, and vision
coverage starting on the **first day of the month following their start date**. The
Company covers **80%** of the employee premium and **50%** of dependent premiums for the
base PPO plan.

## 2. Retirement Plan

Employees are eligible to enroll in the Company's 401(k) plan immediately upon hire. The
Company matches **100% of the first 3%** of employee contributions and **50% of the next
2%**, for a maximum match of **4%**. Matching contributions vest immediately.

## 3. Parental Leave

Eligible employees (6+ months of tenure) receive **12 weeks of paid parental leave**
following the birth, adoption, or fostering of a child. Leave may be taken within 12
months of the qualifying event and can be split into two blocks with manager approval.

## 4. Life and Disability Insurance

The Company provides basic life insurance equal to **1x annual salary** and short-term
disability coverage at no cost to the employee. Supplemental life insurance and long-term
disability are available for voluntary purchase during open enrollment.

## 5. Wellness Benefit

Employees may claim up to **$300 per calendar year** toward gym memberships, fitness
classes, or wellness apps, submitted through the Expense Policy (POL-EXP-001) process
with a "Wellness" category tag.

## 6. Open Enrollment

Benefits elections can be changed annually during the open enrollment period each
November, or within 30 days of a qualifying life event (marriage, birth of a child, loss
of other coverage, etc.).
