# Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** POL-RWK-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All employees in remote-eligible roles

## 1. Overview

Acme Widgets Inc. supports a flexible, hybrid-friendly work environment. This policy
outlines expectations for employees who work remotely on a full-time or part-time basis.

## 2. Eligibility

Remote work eligibility is determined by role. Positions requiring on-site equipment,
in-person client interaction, or physical presence (e.g., warehouse operations) are not
eligible for remote work. Employees should confirm eligibility with their manager and HR.

## 3. Work Arrangements

- **Hybrid**: Employees split time between a Company office and a remote location,
  typically at least 2 days per week on-site.
- **Fully Remote**: Employees work from an approved remote location with no regular
  on-site requirement.
- All arrangements must be documented and approved by the employee's manager and HR.

## 4. Core Hours and Availability

Remote employees must be available and responsive during **core hours of 10:00 AM to
3:00 PM in their assigned time zone**, regardless of their broader schedule. Outside core
hours, employees may exercise flexibility in structuring their workday, subject to team
and manager expectations.

## 5. Equipment and Expenses

- The Company provides a laptop and standard peripherals for remote work.
- Employees may submit expense claims for approved home-office equipment (e.g., monitor,
  chair) up to **$500 per calendar year**, subject to the Expense Policy (POL-EXP-001).
- The Company does not reimburse home internet or utility costs.

## 6. Security Requirements

Remote employees must comply with the Company's Information Security Policy
(POL-SEC-001), including use of Company-approved VPN and device encryption, when
accessing Company systems remotely.

## 7. International Remote Work

Employees wishing to work remotely from a country other than their country of
employment record must obtain prior written approval from HR and Legal, due to tax,
visa, and employment law implications. Unapproved international remote work may result
in disciplinary action.

## 8. Performance Expectations

Remote work status does not change performance expectations. Managers evaluate remote
employees using the same criteria applied to on-site employees.
