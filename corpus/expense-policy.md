# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** POL-EXP-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All employees

## 1. Overview

This policy explains what business expenses are reimbursable and how to submit them.

## 2. General Principles

Expenses must be reasonable, necessary for Company business, and properly documented
with an itemized receipt. The Company does not reimburse expenses that are primarily
personal in nature.

## 3. Travel Expenses

- **Airfare**: Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy permitted for
  flights over 6 hours with manager approval.
- **Lodging**: Reasonable hotel costs, capped at **$250/night** in major metro areas and
  **$180/night** elsewhere, unless a conference rate applies.
- **Ground Transportation**: Taxis, rideshare, and public transit are reimbursable.
  Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate.
- **Meals**: Reimbursed up to **$75/day** while traveling on Company business, itemized
  where possible. Alcohol is reimbursable only when part of a client-facing meal, up to
  2 drinks.

## 4. Home Office Equipment

Remote and hybrid employees may expense home-office equipment up to **$500 per calendar
year**, as referenced in the Remote Work Policy (POL-RWK-001). Eligible items include
monitors, keyboards, chairs, and desks. Recurring costs (internet, electricity) are not
reimbursable.

## 5. Submission Process

1. Submit expenses through the Company expense portal within **30 days** of the
   purchase date.
2. Attach an itemized receipt for any expense over **$25**.
3. Expenses are approved by the employee's direct manager and reviewed by Finance.
4. Approved reimbursements are paid out with the next regular payroll cycle following
   approval.

## 6. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursable: parking or traffic fines, personal entertainment,
gym memberships, in-flight entertainment or wifi upgrades beyond basic connectivity for
work purposes, and expenses lacking a valid receipt.

## 7. Corporate Card Misuse

Use of a Company corporate card for personal expenses, even if later reimbursed to the
Company, may result in card suspension and disciplinary review.
