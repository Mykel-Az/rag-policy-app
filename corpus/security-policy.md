# Information Security Policy

**Document ID:** POL-SEC-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and temporary workers

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the minimum security practices required to protect Company data,
systems, and customer information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

## 2. Account and Password Requirements

- All Company accounts must use **multi-factor authentication (MFA)** where available.
- Passwords must be at least **12 characters**, unique per system, and stored only in the
  Company-approved password manager.
- Passwords must never be shared between employees or written down in plaintext.

## 3. Device Security

- All Company-issued laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and automatic
  screen lock after **5 minutes** of inactivity.
- Personal devices used to access Company email or systems ("BYOD") must be enrolled in
  the Company's mobile device management (MDM) system.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Security within **1 hour** of discovery.

## 4. Data Classification

Company data is classified into three tiers:

1. **Public** – approved for external release (e.g., marketing materials).
2. **Internal** – for employee use only (e.g., internal wikis, org charts).
3. **Confidential** – restricted data including customer PII, financial records, and
   source code. Confidential data may only be shared on a need-to-know basis.

## 5. Remote Access

Remote access to internal systems must go through the Company-approved VPN. Direct
access to production databases from personal networks without VPN is prohibited.

## 6. Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident (phishing email, malware, unauthorized access, data
leak) must be reported immediately to security@acmewidgets.example. Do not attempt to
investigate or remediate incidents independently.

## 7. Third-Party Tools and AI Usage

Employees must not paste Confidential or Internal data into unapproved third-party AI
tools or public generative AI services. Only Company-approved AI tools, configured with
appropriate data-handling agreements, may be used for work involving Confidential data.

## 8. Consequences of Non-Compliance

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including
termination, and may be reported to relevant authorities where required by law.
