# Code of Conduct

**Document ID:** POL-COC-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and Board members

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct sets expectations for professional behavior and ethical decision
making at Acme Widgets Inc.

## 2. Respectful Workplace

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect.
Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation based on race, gender, age, religion,
disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic will not be
tolerated and should be reported to HR immediately via
conduct@acmewidgets.example or the anonymous ethics hotline.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any situation that could create a real or perceived conflict of
interest, including:

- Financial interest in a competitor, supplier, or customer.
- Hiring or supervising a family member without HR approval.
- Accepting gifts from vendors valued over **$100**.

Disclosures should be made to a manager and HR via the annual conflict-of-interest
form or as situations arise.

## 4. Confidentiality

Employees must protect Company Confidential Information (as defined in the Information
Security Policy, POL-SEC-001) both during and after employment. This obligation survives
termination of employment.

## 5. Anti-Bribery and Corruption

Employees may not offer, give, solicit, or accept bribes or improper payments to obtain
or retain business, in compliance with the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and
equivalent local laws.

## 6. Use of Company Resources

Company equipment, systems, and funds must be used for legitimate business purposes.
Incidental personal use of email or internet is permitted within reasonable limits.

## 7. Reporting Violations

Employees who witness a violation of this Code are expected to report it. Retaliation
against anyone who reports a good-faith concern is strictly prohibited and is itself a
violation of this policy.

## 8. Enforcement

Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action up to and including
termination, consistent with applicable law and Company disciplinary procedures.
