# Company Holiday Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HOL-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All employees

## 1. Observed Holidays

Acme Widgets Inc. observes the following **10 paid holidays** each year for
employees in the United States:

1. New Year's Day
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day
3. Memorial Day
4. Juneteenth
5. Independence Day
6. Labor Day
7. Thanksgiving Day
8. Day after Thanksgiving
9. Christmas Eve
10. Christmas Day

Employees in other countries observe the statutory holiday schedule applicable to their
work location, published annually by HR.

## 2. Floating Holidays

In addition to the fixed holidays above, employees receive **2 floating holidays** per
calendar year to use for holidays or observances not listed above (e.g., religious or
cultural holidays). Floating holidays must be used within the calendar year and do not
carry over.

## 3. Holidays Falling on a Weekend

If an observed holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. If it
falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday.

## 4. Holiday Pay for Non-Exempt Employees

Non-exempt employees who are required to work on an observed holiday receive **1.5x**
their regular hourly rate for hours worked that day, in addition to their standard
holiday pay.

## 5. New Hires

Employees are eligible for paid holidays starting on their first day of employment,
regardless of tenure.

## 6. Interaction with PTO

Company holidays do not count against an employee's PTO balance. See the PTO Policy
(POL-PTO-001) for details on requesting time off around holiday periods.
