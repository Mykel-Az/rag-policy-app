# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Document ID:** POL-PTO-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All full-time employees

## 1. Overview

Acme Widgets Inc. ("the Company") provides Paid Time Off (PTO) to eligible employees to
support rest, personal matters, and overall well-being. PTO combines vacation and
personal/sick time into a single flexible bank.

## 2. Eligibility

All full-time employees (working 30+ hours per week) are eligible for PTO starting on their
first day of employment. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis according to
their scheduled hours.

## 3. Accrual

- Employees accrue **1.75 days of PTO per month**, totaling **21 days per year**.
- PTO accrual begins on the employee's start date and is credited at the end of each
  calendar month.
- Unused PTO carries over into the next calendar year up to a maximum of **10 days**.
  Any balance beyond 10 days is forfeited on December 31 unless local law requires
  otherwise.

## 4. Requesting PTO

1. Employees should submit PTO requests through the HR portal at least **5 business days**
   in advance for planned absences.
2. Requests are approved by the employee's direct manager based on team coverage needs.
3. For unplanned absences (e.g., sudden illness), employees should notify their manager
   as soon as reasonably possible, ideally before the start of the workday.

## 5. Unused PTO at Termination

Upon voluntary or involuntary termination, employees will be paid out for any accrued,
unused PTO in accordance with applicable state and local law.

## 6. Extended Leave

PTO is not intended to cover extended medical, parental, or family leave. Employees
requiring leave beyond 5 consecutive PTO days should also refer to the Company's separate
Leave of Absence policy and contact HR to coordinate.

## 7. Holidays

PTO does not need to be used for Company-observed holidays. See the Holiday Policy
(POL-HOL-001) for the list of paid holidays.
